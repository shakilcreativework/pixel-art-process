==================================================
V8.2 — INVISIBLE LATTICE / VISIBLE CELL SEPARATION
==================================================

CRITICAL DISTINCTION:

The global 16 × 16 construction lattice is
INVISIBLE.

It is a hidden mathematical construction system,
NOT a visible design element.

The final artwork must NOT display the construction
grid.

==================================================
NO VISIBLE GRID
==================================================

DO NOT DRAW:

grid lines
cell outlines
grid overlays
square borders
construction guides
reference lines
graph paper
visible lattice lines
debug grid
cell boundary strokes

The 16 × 16 lattice controls geometry internally,
but its lines must NOT appear in the final artwork.

==================================================
VISIBLE ARTWORK = COLORED CELLS ONLY
==================================================

The viewer should see:

colored pixel cells
cell clusters
stepped silhouettes
discrete color regions

The viewer should NOT see:

the underlying grid.

Adjacent cells of the same color should visually merge
into larger continuous shapes.

Do NOT add outlines around every cell.

Do NOT stroke every cell.

Do NOT separate cells with visible borders.

==================================================
CELL BOUNDARIES
==================================================

Cell boundaries exist geometrically but are invisible.

A boundary becomes visually apparent ONLY when:

two neighboring cells have different colors.

If neighboring cells share the same color,
they must visually merge into one continuous region.

==================================================
NO GRID EFFECT
==================================================

The final artwork must NOT resemble:

graph paper
graph paper illustration
spreadsheet cells
tiled UI
checkerboard construction
architectural floor tiles
visible pixel grid

It must resemble authentic high-quality
hand-crafted pixel art.

==================================================
PIXEL ART APPEARANCE
==================================================

Preserve:

hard square pixels
nearest-neighbor appearance
stepped edges
discrete color clusters
clean silhouettes
intentional pixel placement

Avoid:

visible cell outlines
hairline separators
thin strokes
smooth vector geometry
anti-aliasing
subpixel rendering

==================================================
CELL SIZE VS VISUAL SCALE
==================================================

The 16 × 16 base cell remains the smallest
geometric unit.

However, large objects may occupy many adjacent cells.

A mountain may occupy hundreds of cells.

A tree may occupy dozens of cells.

A flower may occupy a small cluster.

A tiny environmental detail may occupy one cell.

The difference between objects comes from
CELL COUNT and CELL ARRANGEMENT.

Never introduce a smaller geometric unit.

==================================================
COLOR CLUSTERING
==================================================

Avoid unnecessary one-cell color noise.

Single cells should be used intentionally.

Prefer:

1-cell accents
2-cell accents
3-cell clusters
4-cell clusters
larger connected clusters

Use isolated single cells only when they have
clear visual purpose.

==================================================
SILHOUETTE PRIORITY
==================================================

When an object becomes too small to represent clearly:

SIMPLIFY THE OBJECT.

Do not shrink the cell.

Do not introduce finer detail.

Preserve:

recognizable silhouette
major color masses
essential features
strong contrast

Remove secondary detail when necessary.

==================================================
DETAIL HIERARCHY
==================================================

BACKGROUND:

large simple cell masses

MIDDLE GROUND:

moderate cell clusters

FOREGROUND:

more cell clusters

FOCAL OBJECTS:

carefully arranged cells with stronger contrast

IMPORTANT:

MORE CELLS

NOT

SMALLER CELLS.

==================================================
FINAL RENDERING RULE
==================================================

Before rendering:

construct everything on the invisible
16 × 16 global lattice.

During rendering:

REMOVE ALL CONSTRUCTION GUIDES.

Final output must show ONLY the pixel artwork.

No grid overlay.

No cell strokes.

No construction lines.

No visible lattice.

==================================================
FINAL QUALITY TEST
==================================================

Ask:

"If the grid lines disappeared completely,
would this still look like intentional,
professional pixel art?"

YES = correct.

NO = redesign the cell clusters.

The viewer should notice the artwork first,
NOT the grid.

==================================================
ABSOLUTE FINAL RULE
==================================================

THE GRID IS A CONSTRUCTION SYSTEM.

THE PIXELS ARE THE ARTWORK.

THE GRID MUST NEVER BECOME THE ARTWORK.
