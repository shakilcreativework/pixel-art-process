NOTES FOR STOCK PRODUCTION
Prioritize the first sets when building your initial silhouette collection.
For each mood, create genuinely distinct poses/gestures rather than near-duplicate submissions.
Strong commercial concepts usually combine the emotion with a clear human action, relationship, situation, or useful silhouette.
Adobe emphasizes authentic emotion, connection, relatable everyday moments, diverse representation, and commercially useful imagery.
Metadata matters: Adobe says the first 10 keywords have the greatest influence on search ranking, so use the most important and accurate concepts first.

SOURCE BASIS
- Adobe Stock 2026 Creative Trends: All the Feels, Connectioneering, Surreal Silliness, and Local Flavor.
- Adobe Stock contributor guidance: authentic people, emotional expressions, connection, compassion, leadership, everyday moments, and commercially useful content.
- Adobe's 2025 Firefly emotion-prompt analysis: happiness, serenity, confident, relaxation, and joy were the top five positive/calming emotions; confident, nostalgia, and relaxation were among the top trending terms.
- Adobe Stock's Emotions & Feelings discovery taxonomy includes happiness, sadness, loneliness, anger, inspiration, calm, peace, hope, fear, gratitude, joy, anxiety, surprise, empathy, excitement, confidence, pride, disappointment, regret, shame, euphoria, guilt, contentment, melancholy, anticipation, and longing.
