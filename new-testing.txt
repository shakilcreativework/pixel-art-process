02 — ONE LATTICE / CELL OCCUPANCY LOGIC
======================================================================

Treat the entire artwork as a large discrete grid.

Every grid position is either:

EMPTY

OCCUPIED.

An occupied region must consist of complete cells.

Construct the artwork using this sequence:

CONCEPT
→ CELL OCCUPANCY
→ CELL CLUSTERS
→ SILHOUETTES
→ OBJECTS
→ SCENE
→ FINAL PIXEL ART

Every curve must be a stepped cell structure.
